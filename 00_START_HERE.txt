🚀 5-MINUTE OVERVIEW

Your data flow:

  ESP32 Firmware (30 sec)
        ↓
  LoRa Gateway
        ↓
  TTN Network (uses TTN_PAYLOAD_FORMATTER.js)
        ↓
  Vercel Function (ttn-webhook.js)
        ↓
  Supabase PostgreSQL (stores in sensor_data table)
        ↓
  React Dashboard (real-time updates, no refresh needed!)
        ↓
  Your Browser

Total latency: < 10 seconds!
